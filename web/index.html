10 golden rules

  1. Banks never ask for OTP, PIN or CVV.
  2. Never pay to receive a prize or refund.
  3. Verify parcel fees on the official courier app.
  4. Type government and bank domains yourself.
  5. Do not scan a QR code to “receive” money.
  6. Never install an APK or remote-control app on request.
  7. Do not stay on a police or CBI video call.
  8. Ignore urgent threats that demand secrecy.
  9. Check the sender domain, not just the display name.
  10. Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
